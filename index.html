What is stored, and who can read it

There is no place anywhere in MoneyMap to type a card number, PIN, CVV, expiry date or bank password. The app never asks for them, and the database has no column that could hold one.

Signed out: everything stays in this browser only and nothing is sent anywhere.
Signed in with Google: your wallets and records are also saved to your own private rows in the MoneyMap database, so they survive a lost phone and follow you to another device. Row level security means those rows can only ever be read by your Google account — another signed-in user asking the database for them gets nothing back, and a signed-out visitor is refused outright.

How to use it

  1. In Wallets, add each place your money sits: cash in your pocket, cash at home, a card in USD, a card in KGS.
  2. In Rates, check the numbers so different currencies can be summed into one total.
  3. In Spending, write every record: how much, from which wallet, and where it went.
  4. The Overview then shows your total money, cash vs card, each currency, and your biggest spending places.
  5. Optionally sign in with Google — the same book then opens on your phone and your laptop, and a lost device costs you nothing.
