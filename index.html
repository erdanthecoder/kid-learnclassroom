    What is stored, and who can read it

    There is no place anywhere in Vaultline to type a card number, PIN, CVV, expiry date or bank password. It never asks for them, and the database has no field that could hold one.

    Signed out — everything stays in this browser and nothing is sent anywhere.
    Signed in with Google — your wallets and records are also kept in your own documents in Firebase, so a lost phone costs you nothing and the same book opens on your laptop. The security rule allows a signed-in person to touch their own documents and nothing else.

    How to use it

    1. In Wallets, add each place your money sits: cash in your pocket, cash at home, a card in som, a card in dollars.
    2. In Rates, check the numbers so different currencies can be added into one total.
    3. In Records, write what you spent, from which wallet, and where it went.
    4. In Budgets, set a monthly limit for the categories you want to keep an eye on.
    5. Vault then shows everything you have, cash against card, each currency, and what stands out this month.
